Health & Safety Guidelines

To keep our baby boy safe and healthy, we kindly ask all guests to observe the following guidelines during the event:

  • No Kissing the Baby: Please refrain from kissing the baby on the face, hands, or cheeks.
  • No Smoking or Vaping: Strictly no smoking or vaping near the baby or event area.
  • Hand Hygiene: Alcohol and hand sanitizers are a must—please sanitize your hands before interacting with or touching the baby.
  • Stay Home if Unwell: If you are feeling under the weather or experiencing any symptoms, please celebrate with us from home.
Please follow protocols for everyone's safety.
